Rocks & Gemstones of Scotland

Scotland's beaches, hills and riverbanks are full of rocks and minerals you can collect. Here are some of the most popular finds.

What you can find

Beach find

Agate

Banded, translucent pebbles with stripes or rings. Look for smooth, rounded stones that glow when wet.

Where: east coast beaches, such as Lunan Bay in Angus
Highland

Cairngorm (smoky quartz)

A brownish-grey quartz named after the Cairngorm Mountains. Usually found as crystals in rock.

Where: the Highlands, especially the Cairngorms
Very common

Quartz

One of the most common minerals in Scotland. Look for white or milky pebbles and clear crystals in streams and on hillsides.

Where: rivers, streams and hillsides everywhere
Notable

Serpentine

A green, waxy rock with mottled patterns. It polishes beautifully when you find a good piece.

Where: Unst in Shetland
Highland

Garnet

Small red-brown crystals set in schist rock. Look for shiny, rounded grains in the rock face.

Where: schist rock in parts of the Highlands

Tips for collecting

  • Wet stones show their colours and patterns much better.
  • Check local rules before you collect, especially on nature reserves and Sites of Special Scientific Interest (SSSIs).
  • Only take small amounts for personal use and leave the rest for others.
  • Be careful on cliffs and rocky shores, and check the tide times.
Happy hunting!
